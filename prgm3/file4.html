Department of MCA

Ilahia College of Engineering and Technology (ICET) focuses on educating and preparing students to become dynamic IT professionals equipped for the global tech market. Established in 2008 with a standard intake of 60 students, the department emphasizes a strong balance between theoretical computer science and practical, real-world experience. The core curriculum of the two-year postgraduate program includes intensive laboratory work, specialized classrooms, and an entirely dedicated final-semester project.
